v1.2.0 · Android offline voice log

Offline voice notes that sort themselves into tasks.

Record up to two minutes on Android, transcribe speech locally with Whistle, and extract a short title, tags, and checkable todos into SQLite with no cloud account.

Download APK (v1.2.0)

Android arm64-v8a · ~93 MB one-time model cache · Runs locally after setup

Pipeline

Three local steps from thumb press to SQLite

No audio stream, transcript, or note leaves the device. Network access happens once at setup to download and SHA-256 verify the model weights.

  1. 01 / CAPTURE

    Hold to record, swipe to lock

    Hold the bottom mic button for quick thoughts up to 120 seconds, swipe up to lock hands-free, slide left to discard, or type a note directly.

  2. 02 / SPEECH

    On-device speech with Whistle

    Kotlin MediaCodec resamples recordings to 16 kHz mono PCM with Hann-windowed sinc interpolation. Fine-tuned Svarah whistle.cact (29.3 MB) transcribes 30-second windows with 1.5-second overlap, 6-word boundary deduplication, and custom Vault keywords.

  3. 03 / EXTRACTION

    Local task extraction with Needle 3

    Fine-tuned needle3.cact (63.4 MB, W4A8) invokes save_note over C++ JNI to emit a headline, boolean category flags (#bug, #ship, #idea, #people, #study), pending todos (#todo), or #log when uncategorized.

Boundaries

Storage, privacy, and honest limits

On-device storage

  • Notes and .m4a clips live in expo-sqlite and local app storage with android:allowBackup="false".
  • Switch between Notes and Tasks views, filter by keyword, #tag, or #open, and review the 14-day cadence grid in Vault.
  • Export everything as JSON anytime through Android Storage Access Framework.

Known boundaries

  • First-time model setup downloads roughly 93 MB from Hugging Face before offline extraction starts; notes recorded before download queue as raw transcripts.
  • Merging the local LoRA drops Needle 3's confidence head, so confidence reads null and 11.1% false positives remain on non-task phrasing.
  • Targets 64-bit Android (arm64-v8a). 32-bit armeabi-v7a is not bundled in this APK; voice clips cap at 120 seconds.
